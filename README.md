# Europe Climate Extremes Analysis

A collaborative data analysis project investigating how global warming has affected the frequency and intensity of extreme heat events in Europe, and how their probability and return periods may change under future climate scenarios.

## Research Question

**How has global warming affected the frequency and intensity of extreme heat events in Europe, and how might their probability and return periods change under future climate scenarios?**

## Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- analyse long-term temperature trends across Europe;
- investigate changes in the frequency and intensity of extreme heat events;
- apply statistical methods to determine whether observed trends are significant;
- model extreme temperatures using Extreme Value Theory;
- estimate probabilities and return periods of extreme heat events;
- quantify uncertainty using Bayesian methods;
- investigate how extreme-heat risk may change under future climate scenarios;
- evaluate possible heat-adaptation strategies.

## Methodology

The project will include:

- descriptive statistics;
- moving averages and distribution analysis;
- Mann–Kendall trend testing;
- linear regression;
- confidence intervals;
- Generalized Extreme Value (GEV) modelling;
- return-level and return-period estimation;
- Bayesian uncertainty analysis;
- future climate scenario analysis.
